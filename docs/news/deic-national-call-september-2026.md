# DeiC national resource pool: apply by 1 September 2026

Has your research project outgrown your computer's capacity, or do our local facilities no longer meet your computational needs?

DeiC has opened the call for applications for access to compute resources at the national HPC facilities, including the Danish share of EuroHPC [LUMI](/external-hpc/lumi/). The application round is open until **1 September 2026**.

These calls only open twice a year, so now is a good time to consider whether your project could benefit from applying. Researchers at Aalborg University — and other Danish universities — can apply for access to these resources.

## How to apply

Read the call text, find the application guide, and submit your application through [DeiC's website](https://www.deic.dk/en/supercomputing-hpc/get-started/apply-computing-power).

For an overview of the national resource pool, timelines, and which platforms you can apply to, see our guide on [applications to the national resource pool](/external-hpc/deic-resources/#applications-to-the-national-resource-pool).

!!! questions "Questions about your application?"

    If you have questions about applying for a national grant, reach out to CLAAUDIA via [serviceportal.aau.dk](https://aau.service-now.com/serviceportal?id=sc_cat_item&sys_id=34e8536083cfc21053711d447daad30a).
