# Online session: AI for research at AAU

**28 October 2026, 14:30–15:30**, online.

The session is for researchers at Aalborg University. It is held in Danish, with live English subtitles. No registration is required.

Mistral can open new possibilities in research. This hour looks at how to use it responsibly, and what to consider when the work involves sensitive data.

Mathias Hedegaard, Data Scientist at CLAAUDIA, introduces Mistral and shows how researchers at AAU can use it as a support tool. He helped implement Mistral at AAU and will cover safe use, plus the requirements that apply to sensitive data.

Researchers can use Mistral to analyse data, process research materials, automate repetitive tasks, and test new methods. At AAU it is available both as a chatbot and through an API, so it can sit inside your own tools and workflows.

## In the session

- An introduction to Mistral.
- Practical examples of how Mistral can be used in research.
- What is possible with sensitive data, and which requirements you need to consider.
